Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common buyer questions regarding plot sizes, approvals, location, and registry.

1. What plot sizes are available in this Kanpur Road township? ▼
Plot sizes available are 1000 sq.ft (20x50), 1200 sq.ft (24x50), 1500 sq.ft (30x50), and 2000 sq.ft (40x50). All plots have approved demarcation and are ready for villa construction.
2. What villa configurations are offered? ▼
We offer 3 BHK Independent Villas starting from 1800 sq.ft built-up area (G+1 duplex) and 4 BHK Premium Duplex Villas starting from 2400 sq.ft built-up area with private terrace and covered parking.
3. What is the starting price for plots and villas? ▼
Residential plots start at ₹65 Lakh onwards. Independent villas start at ₹1.15 Cr onwards depending on floor plan and plot facing option.
4. Is this project RERA registered and bank loan eligible? ▼
Yes, the project is fully RERA approved (RERA Reg No: UPRERAPRJ123456). Up to 80% bank financing is available from SBI, HDFC, ICICI, and PNB.
5. How far is Lucknow Airport and Shaheed Path from the township? ▼
CCS International Airport is just 10 minutes away, Shaheed Path Expressway is 10 minutes away, and the Outer Ring Road interchange is just 5 minutes from the township gate.
6. Are there any high-rise apartments or flats in this project? ▼
No. This is an exclusive low-density residential township dedicated strictly to independent plots and villas. There are zero high-rise apartments.
